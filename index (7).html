Twisting Nether

Internal outreach toolbox for a small team — Google Sheets add-ons (Twisting Nether panel, TN Control Center) and the TN Warmup Agent web app.

Twisting Nether helps our team manage its own outreach mailboxes: it reads the team's own Google Sheets, shows replies and deliverability signals, and (Warmup Agent) exchanges short internal messages between the team's own mailboxes to keep them healthy.

The apps are used only by members of our team under their own Google accounts. They are not offered to the public.
